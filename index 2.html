Our Story

A neighbourhood barber with a craftsman’s soul.

Nkomo Cutz started with a single chair, a pair of clippers, and a simple belief: every man deserves to leave looking and feeling like the best version of himself. More than a decade later, that belief still guides every fade, trim, and shave.

We blend old-school discipline with a modern, design-led eye. No rushed appointments, no shortcuts — just sharp lines, honest advice, and a grooming ritual you’ll look forward to every few weeks.

Andile Nkomo Founder & Master Barber
