The barber behind the brand

A young hand with an old-soul obsession for detail.

Andile Nkomo started with a single pair of clippers and a refusal to do anything halfway. What began as cuts for friends became Nkomo Cutz — a young grooming brand built on one belief: a great cut isn't a service, it's a statement.

Every fade is measured. Every line is intentional. Every client leaves looking like the campaign they're about to star in. No rushing, no shortcuts, no two heads treated the same.
